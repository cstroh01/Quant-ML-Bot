## What N counts

Lifetime N is the immutable approved backfill count plus every subsequent candidate
start. Duplicate configurations, failures, abandoned attempts and interrupted runs
still count: selecting among attempts can inflate apparent performance even when
some attempts never finish. Baselines retain their separate roles. Verification
binds the count to one ledger head; a damaged chain does not yield a usable count.

A valid preregistered family may separately use its family count under spec 058's
conditions. Lifetime DSR is still published. Breaching the family's conditions or
cap removes that exception; the family count cannot silently replace lifetime N.

## Why backfill is upper-biased

The backfill takes defensible upper bounds on campaign combinations and reruns,
adds separate campaigns, rounds upward to a power of two, then doubles. Immutable
supersession cannot lower the effective count. Missing history is not zero trials:
an unresolved/unbounded campaign makes the backfill incomplete and blocks a pass.

## Why M differs from N

M counts eligible candidate return series with verified sidecars and the required
shared OOS dates, costs and CV metadata. N counts selection opportunities. A failed
attempt or historical backfill can increase N without supplying any return series.
Inventing a column for such an attempt would fabricate evidence; using M as N
would erase selection history and make DSR too permissive.

## How DSR and PBO share evidence

Both statistics use one deterministically built and hashed return matrix. DSR also
uses the verified selection count and declared moments/conventions. PBO uses its
CSCV analysis and is mandatory diagnostic evidence, without a numeric pass cutoff.
Gate 3 requires DSR at least 0.95 and HAC t-statistic at least 3, inclusively,
alongside valid current provenance and PBO. The API reads and assesses immutable
evidence; it does not rerun these statistics.

## Why a new trial makes old evidence stale

A new start changes the ledger head and potentially N and matrix eligibility.
The currentness helper verifies the current ledger and rebuilds the matrix identity
from its bound sidecars and immutable family policy. An old artifact cannot remain
passed by repeating its own saved hashes. The reader also verifies the latest index
entry's artifact digest and metadata; an invalid latest entry cannot fall back to
an older passing artifact.

## What the planted defect proves

The fixed synthetic strategy has the same returns, costs and non-history inputs
at N=88 and N=46. It fails the 0.95 DSR gate at 88 and passes at 46, demonstrating
that selection history affects the actual route result. Isolated mutations weaken
the threshold, substitute matrix-column count for lifetime N, or skip the artifact
digest check. Each is caught while clean controls pass. These proofs establish
that the tested controls detect those specific faults; they do not establish
investment edge, current production Gate 3 acceptance or broker readiness.
