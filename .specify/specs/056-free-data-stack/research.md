## Decisions and unresolved evidence

- Pricing-free, research-permitted, public-redistributable and PIT-correct are four separate gates. Fail closed on any unverified requirement; never trade one for another.
- The provider-page comparison changes no repository scope or disclosure. §9 already lists Tiingo/Alpaca candidates; the proposed primary ordering above favors an explicit raw/SIP contract. Human verification, not this document, adopts it.
- Do not contact vendors, register keys, download data, change current cache, or weaken 044 volume gates in this lane. Provider-documented volume basis is a future adapter contract; it does not establish the basis of yfinance rows.
- French source data built from CRSP does not authorize a paid-feed dependency. No paid vendor or tier recommended.
- Official pages were directly opened today, with `find` used to inspect licensing/basis/limits. Failed routes: Stooq `/t/` empty; Tiingo `/about/terms` failed then official pricing terms link resolved `/tos`; EODHD `/terms-of-use` 404 then docs link resolved; SEC `/about/website-policies` 404; Treasury privacy route inaccessible; OpenFIGI guessed terms route inaccessible. These failures are not evidence of permissive licenses.
